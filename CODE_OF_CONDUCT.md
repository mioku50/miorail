# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be straight with people and assume they are being straight with you.

Technical disagreement is welcome and expected — this project is about
measurement, and measurements are worth arguing over. Argue about the claim,
the evidence, and the code. Not about the person making them.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome
attention, publishing someone's private information, or sustained disruption.

Two things that matter more here than in most projects:

- **Do not misrepresent evidence.** Fabricating a measurement, a benchmark or a
  reproduction — in an issue, a pull request or a review — is treated as
  seriously as any conduct issue on this list.
- **Do not weaponise security reports.** Reporting a vulnerability to get
  attention, or disclosing one early to apply pressure, harms users who had no
  part in the disagreement.

## Scope

Applies in this repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to **<speedhall50@gmail.com>**. Reports are read by the maintainer and
kept confidential. Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban,
proportionate to what happened and whether it continued after being raised.

If you disagree with an enforcement decision, say so in writing; it will be
reconsidered once, by the same person, with your argument in front of them.
